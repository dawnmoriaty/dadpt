2.  Product  Background  
Technology  is  advancing  at  an  accelerating  pace,  and  with  it  the  imperative  for  digital  transformation  
is
 
gaining
 
prominence.
 
Education—despite
 
its
 
strategic
 
importance—has
 
yet
 
to
 
adopt
 
digital
 
transformation
 
in
 
a
 
robust
 
and
 
systematic
 
manner.
 
Based
 
on
 
a
 
clear-eyed
 
appraisal
 
of
 
current
 
conditions,
 
together
 
with
 
market
 
analysis
 
and
 
user-needs
 
research,
 
our
 
team
 
has
 
identified
 
digital
 
libraries
 
as
 
a
 
salient
 
focus
 
area.
 
Although
 
numerous
 
solutions
 
exist,
 
they
 
remain
 
fragmented,
 
insufficiently
 
comprehensive,
 
and
 
often
 
fail
 
to
 
address
 
several
 
core
 
issues.
 
Accordingly,  we  will  research  and  develop  a  digital-library  solution  that  elevates  document  quality  
and
 
places
 
the
 
user
 
experience
 
at
 
the
 
centre.
 
By
 
leveraging
 
artificial
 
intelligence,
 
the
 
platform
 
will
 
augment
 
human
 
effort,
 
provide
 
a
 
richer
 
corpus
 
of
 
high-quality
 
references,
 
and
 
enhance
 
the
 
objectivity
 
and
 
reliability
 
of
 
the
 
system
 
as
 
a
 
whole.

-  StuDocu  _  Link  website -  Viblo  _  Link  website -  Course  Hero  _  Link  website -  Docsity  _  Link  website -  Academia  _   Link  website -  Scribd  _  Link  website -  TailieuVN  _  Link  website

4.  Business  Opportunity  
Upon  release,  the  platform  is  designed  to  deliver  a  solution  that  marries  technological  sophistication  
with
 
demonstrable
 
quality.
 
For  the  platform  itself.  It  will  operate  as  a  high-assurance  environment  in  which  all  submissions  
undergo
 
rigorous,
 
front-door
 
moderation.
 
This
 
creates
 
an
 
independent,
 
well-curated
 
repository
 
with
 
clear
 
provenance,
 
robust
 
quality
 
controls,
 
and
 
auditable
 
standards.
 
For  end-users.  Participants  will  engage  in  a  tightly  governed,  independent  ecosystem  where  content  
quality
 
and
 
user
 
safety
 
are
 
paramount.
 
The
 
experience
 
prioritises
 
reliability,
 
transparency,
 
and
 
access
 
to
 
trustworthy
 
materials.
 
For  authors.  Contributors  will  work  within  an  impartial,  standards-driven  setting  and  benefit  from  
meaningful
 
incentives—recognition,
 
analytics,
 
and
 
merit-based
 
visibility—while
 
retaining
 
clarity
 
over
 
rights
 
and
 
attribution.
 
For  institutions.  The  platform  functions  as  both  an  archival  store  and  a  collaborative  hub  for  learning  
and
 
development,
 
improving
 
educational
 
quality
 
while
 
exemplifying
 
a
 
socially
 
responsible,
 
sustainable
 
business
 
model.
 
For  experts.  Subject-matter  specialists  can  leverage  their  expertise  to  raise  the  bar  on  content  quality  
through
 
review,
 
curation,
 
and
 
targeted
 
enhancement
 
workflows.
 
In  summary,  The  system  aspires  to  be  a  multi-role,  highly  connected  platform  that  supports  diverse  
use
 
cases
 
and
 
operating
 
models—yet
 
consistently
 
upholds
 
discipline,
 
rigorous
 
oversight,
 
and
 
careful
 
control
 
without
 
compromising
 
user
 
benefits
 
or
 
experience. 
5.  Software  Product  Vision  
This  digital  library  aims  to  establish  a  rigorously  structured  platform  that  tightly  couples  documents  
with
 
their
 
semantic
 
representations
.
 
All
 
document
 
operations
 
must
 
remain
 
fully
 
consistent
 
with
 
their
 
summaries,
 
content
 
tags,
 
and
 
user-defined
 
classifications,
 
ensuring
 
end-to-end
 
coherence.
 
To  maximise  this  ambition,  the  system  will  first  complete  and  harden  core  user  interactions .  It  will  
then
 
deploy
 
user-action
 
recognition
 
to
 
capture
 
behavioural
 
signals
 
for
 
analysis
 
and
 
continuous
 
improvement.
 
Crucially,  artificial  intelligence  will  be  embedded  as  a  first-class  pillar  of  the  operating  
strategy—reflecting
 
prevailing
 
industry
 
trends—to
 
increase
 
accuracy,
 
technological
 
depth,
 
and
 
objective
 
quality
 
without
 
compromising
 
user
 
experience
 
or
 
functional
 
breadth.
 
We  will  begin  by  finalising  user-facing  features,  then  introduce  AI  selectively  into  high-impact  
capabilities
.
 
Over
 
time,
 
AI
 
will
 
be
 
progressively
 
extended
 
across
 
all
 
workflows
,
 
while

human-in-the-loop
 
oversight
 
for

to

6.  Project  Scope  &  Limitations  
In  this  first  development  cycle,  the  platform  prioritises  foundational  user  capabilities—covering  core  
interactions
 
with
 
the
 
system
 
and
 
with
 
documents.
 
Administration  
●  A  comprehensive  admin  console  provides  management  and  reporting  across  key  domains:  
security-related
 
events,
 
user
 
traffic
 
and
 
engagement,
 
document-quality
 
indicators,
 
and
 
user-created
 
contacts.
 
 ●  Logs  and  metrics  are  presented  with  clear,  actionable  detail  to  support  oversight  and  
continuous
 
improvement.
 
Organisational  Workspaces  
●  Participating  organisations  operate  within  dedicated,  isolated  workspaces  (multi-tenant  by  
design).
 
 ●  Per-document  public/  private  visibility  can  be  configured,  and  each  organisation  may  
establish
 
its
 
own
 
management
 
team
 
and
 
governance
 
rules.
 
AI  Enablement  
●  From  the  outset,  the  release  integrates  AI-assisted  moderation  to  assess  the  quality  of  
uploaded
 
documents
 
before
 
publication.
 
 ●  It  also  offers  tri-level  summarisation  for  readers:  
 1.  Quick  gist  (very  brief),  
 2.  Standard  summary ,  
 3.  Detailed  summary  (more  comprehensive  and  structured).  
 
This  combination  delivers  a  disciplined  baseline  of  functionality  while  laying  the  groundwork  for  
robust
 
curation,
 
accountability,
 
and
 
a
 
high-quality
 
reading
 
experience.
